# SkorKeeper Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026

SkorKeeper ("the app") is developed by Schlosser Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy
Policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

## Summary

SkorKeeper is an **offline-first** app. You do not need to create an account, and no
gameplay data, scores, or personal information is ever transmitted to or stored on our
servers. All game history, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device.

The only data collection that occurs is described below, and comes entirely from
third-party services we use for advertising and in-app purchases.

## Information Collected by Third-Party Services

### Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of SkorKeeper displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure
ads, Google AdMob may collect:

- Advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA)
- Device information (device type, operating system version)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

On iOS, the app requests your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT)
framework before any tracking-related data is used for ad personalization. You can decline
this prompt and still use the app; you will see non-personalized ads instead.

Users who purchase the ad-free upgrade (SkorKeeper Pro) or a Sports Tier do not see ads and
are not subject to AdMob's data collection.

See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how AdMob handles data:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### In-App Purchases (RevenueCat, Apple, Google)

SkorKeeper offers optional one-time in-app purchases (Pro unlock, Sports tiers, Tip Jar).
Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play; we never see or store
your payment card details.

To verify and restore purchases across devices, purchase and entitlement data (such as a
device/app-specific purchaser ID and purchase receipts) is shared with RevenueCat, our
in-app purchase management provider. RevenueCat does not receive any payment card
information.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not use any analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs of our own. We do not
collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not require
account creation or sign-in.

## Children's Privacy

SkorKeeper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through
a third-party service used by the app, please contact us so we can address it.

## Data Retention & Your Choices

Because all gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by
uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings. For data held by AdMob or
RevenueCat, refer to their respective privacy policies for how to exercise access/deletion
rights.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page
with an updated effective date.
