# Data Deletion Request

SkorKeeper is developed by Schlosser Studio.

SkorKeeper does not use user accounts, so there is no account to delete or sign in to. All gameplay data, history, notes, and counters are stored locally on your device.

To delete your data:

1. Open the app and delete any history entries, notes, or counters you want removed.
2. Remove the app from your device, or clear the app's storage/data in your device settings.

What is deleted:

- Game history and saved scores
- Notepad entries
- Tally counters
- Local app settings and preferences

What may be kept:

- Purchase records, receipts, and entitlement data handled by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat, which are governed by their own retention policies
- Ad-related data handled by Google AdMob, which is governed by Google's retention policies

If you need help with deletion, contact us at schlosser.joe24@gmail.com.
